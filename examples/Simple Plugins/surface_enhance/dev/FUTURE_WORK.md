# Surface enhancement follow-ups

The first supported workflow is registered monochrome stacks under known
directional lights and declared linear radiometry. Retained source setup, region
editing, independent previews, transactional staging and cached execution are
available. Its examples are labeled rendered controls, not physical qualification.

- Acquire a small explicitly licensed calibrated real light stack with normal,
  shape or independently annotated inspection references. The dataset audit
  records access and large solid-archive constraints; request a publisher subset
  or deliberately schedule larger downloads. Keep exact originals/attribution.
- Validate camera linearity, black drift, exposure changes, motion and registration
  on acquired images. Add guided dark/flat/sphere calibration rather than assuming
  lighting directions or learning calibration from an unknown inspection part.
- Add near-field/spatially varying lighting, shadow visibility and richer material
  models. Current residuals indicate diffuse-model disagreement; they do not
  classify specularity, shadows, scratches or defects.
- Improve relighting controls, ROI shapes/polygons, light-by-light residual/used
  observation overlays, and acquisition-sequence setup with frame-order/exposure
  metadata checks. Preserve imported masks and Apply/Cancel semantics.
- Profile tiled execution across representative light counts/sizes before bounded
  multicore/GPU/streaming changes. Preserve numerical/support/reason parity and
  avoid serializing image stacks as JSON or recompiling lighting per frame.
- Add actual surface inspection examples combining these maps with Segmentation
  or ReferenceInspect. Enhancement alone does not decide acceptance.
- Consider metric height integration only with stated physical assumptions,
  spatial scale, anchors and independent dimensional references. Never label a
  normal RGB map as a measured point cloud.

The follow-ups remain open in the tool roadmap.
