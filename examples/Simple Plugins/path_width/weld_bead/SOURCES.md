# Image and mask sources

Hyun Soo Kim (2024), *Weld bead images and masks*, Figshare v1, archive file
`46550428`. The three original PNG images and their matching `_mask.png`
files in `assets/` retain their upstream filenames and bytes. The saved
`weld.spw` contains a grayscale copy of
`20230930_21h03m42s_B11_1.png` as its training reference.
